How to use the training app

Your coach has prepared a plan that opens on your phone. In a few minutes you'll see how to use it — just watch the video or scroll down.

In short

  1. Open the link from your coach and add it to your home screen.
  2. Tap Next workout.
  3. Train from the list or let the app guide you set by set.
  4. Rate the exercises, and if the weight was different from the plan — enter it.
  5. At the end, tap Finish workout.

1

Open your plan

Your coach will send you a link. It opens in your phone's browser — no account to create and nothing to install from an app store.

  • Your next workout is always waiting at the top — tap it to start.
  • Below are the weeks of the cycle. The dots show how many workouts of each week you've done.
  • The same link works for every cycle — a new plan from your coach will appear in it by itself.
  • You can collapse the tip cards at the top to a single line or remove them (✕). All this information is also always in the “More” section at the bottom of the main page.

The link is just for you: anyone who has it can see your plan. Don't pass it on.

2

Add it to your home screen

Once, the first time you open it. After that you open your workouts like a regular app — full screen, with no hunting for the link in your messages.

  • iPhone (Safari): tap the share icon (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen and Add.
  • Android (Chrome): menu ⋮, then Add to Home screen (or “Install app”).

Removed the tip in the app with ✕? The same information is always in the “More” section at the bottom of the main page — Add to home screen.

3

Where your weights come from

If your coach doesn't know your weights for some exercises yet, you'll see the card “Where your weights come from”. There are two ways:

  • Start training right away. Such an exercise shows “choose” instead of kilograms. Pick a weight by RPE (below) and log your first set — the weight and as many reps as you actually did. The app will calculate the weights for the rest of the plan from it.
  • Max sets first. A separate session: one set to failure for each exercise, with good technique. You enter the weight and the number of reps.

When choosing the weight, you don't have to stick to the planned number of reps. The plan says: 8 reps at RPE 8 (2 in reserve). You picked a weight, got 10 reps and still feel 2 more would go? Enter 10. The app will treat the weight as lighter than the plan assumes and raise the weights for the next sets and weeks — you'll see it straight away in the faded suggestions for the next sets (e.g. 32.5 kg × 8 after 30 kg × 10). What matters is finishing the set with as much in reserve as the plan says (RPE).

Max sets are also always at the bottom of the main page, in the “More” section.

4

What is RPE

RPE tells you how hard a set should be. The easiest way is to count how many reps you have left in reserve — how many more you could do cleanly if you didn't stop.

RPE 10nothing in reserve — you couldn't do another RPE 91 in reserve RPE 82 in reserve RPE 73 in reserve RPE 64 or more

Halves sit in between: RPE 7.5 means 2–3 in reserve. Example: “8 reps · RPE 8” is a weight where after the eighth rep you feel two more would still go.

5

Training from the list

After tapping “Next workout” you see all of the day's exercises: weight, sets, reps and RPE.

  • A1, B1, B2… — exercises with the same letter are a superset: you alternate them (a set of B1, a set of B2, rest, and again). A shared edge on the left links them.
  • ▶ video shows the exercise technique.
  • Warm-up ramp — a few lighter sets before the first working set. You don't log these sets.
  • You can change the order: ▶ Start this exercise is next to each one.
6

Rate the exercise and log what you did

After the exercise, tap one of the ratings:

  • Too hard — the coming weeks will be lighter.
  • OK — stays as your coach planned.
  • Too easy — the coming weeks will be heavier.

Exercises without a rating are saved as “OK”.

You don't have to log the weights — a rating is enough. Log a set when the weight was different from the plan (and always with “choose”): the app will recalculate the rest of the plan from that set. Log sets manually under the rating opens the set fields. Enter what you actually did — including a different number of reps than planned (example next to this: planned 8, done 10). The faded numbers in the next rows are a suggestion from the previous set — after the set, tap ✓ or correct them.

7

Or: guided set by set

▶ Guide me set by set shows one set at a time: weight, reps, RPE and warm-up. Handy at the gym — nothing to look for.

  • Different weight or number of reps? Correct the numbers on the screen.
  • Too heavy or too light? Tap Too hard or Too easy. If it was OK — don't tap anything.
  • Then Finish set — the rest timer starts by itself. You can skip it or add 30 seconds.
  • The A1, B1… buttons at the top take you to any exercise, and “Whole day as a list” takes you back to the list.
8

Finish the workout

At the bottom of the list, tap Finish workout. The progress bar on the main page moves on, and the next workout appears at the top.

No signal at the gym? The app keeps working. At the top you'll see the bar “Offline — I'll save when you're back online”, and your entries will be sent by themselves once your phone is back online.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to log every set?

No. Rating the exercise is enough. Log a set when the weight was different from the plan, and for exercises with “choose” — the coming weeks are then calculated from your set.

I chose a weight and got more reps than planned. What do I enter?

What you actually did. The plan says 8 reps at RPE 8, you got 10 and still had 2 in reserve — enter the weight and 10. The app will recalculate the weights upwards. The same the other way: you got 6 with the same reserve — enter 6, and the next weights will be lighter.

Can I train on other days or do the exercises in a different order?

Yes. The plan is a sequence of workouts, not calendar days — “Next workout” always shows the first one you haven't done. Within a workout you can start with any exercise.

I forgot to tap “Finish workout”.

Nothing is lost — your ratings and logged sets are already saved. Open that workout from the list of weeks and tap “Finish workout”.

The video won't play.

Technique videos play from the internet — you need a signal. The rest of the app also works offline.

I've lost the link.

Message your coach — it's the same link as always and works for every cycle.

When will I see my next plan?

When your coach has prepared it — it will appear under the same link. Until then you see the last plan you received.

Questions about your plan or the exercises? Message your coach. Have a great workout!
